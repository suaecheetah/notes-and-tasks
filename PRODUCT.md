# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Pioneers and designers with millions of plans on their minds. They need a space to manage complex, multifaceted ideas while keeping their workflow visually fluid.

## Product Purpose
To keep creators both creative and organized. It provides a structured checklist paired with an open-ended, infinite whiteboard canvas for each task, allowing ideas to flow without losing track of what needs to be done.

## Brand Personality
Playful, Helping, Creative. The interface should feel like an inspiring, dynamic workspace rather than a rigid management tool.

## Anti-references
Should explicitly NOT look like a generic corporate business website or sterile B2B SaaS tool. Avoid overly formal, rigid, or highly-constrained layouts.

## Design Principles
- **Structure meets freedom:** Pair organized, actionable lists with boundless canvases.
- **Creator-first:** Design for visual thinkers; keep interactions playful, helpful, and intuitive.
- **Detailed but open:** Support deep documentation (like a wiki) within a freeform, highly visual environment (like Figma).

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Support deep personalization: Users must be able to change the theme color dynamically (e.g., via a color wheel) and access ready-to-use presets to accommodate their sensory preferences.
- Maintain sufficient contrast and legibility across all custom color choices.
